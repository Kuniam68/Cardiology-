EVERYDAY HEART HEALTH

Small habits.
Lasting attention.

Discuss appropriate physical activity, balanced meals, tobacco cessation, sleep, and blood pressure monitoring with your healthcare professional. Do not change prescribed medication without medical advice.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a referral?

Referral requirements depend on the clinic and your coverage. Confirm with the clinic before attending.

Should I fast before my visit?

Only fast if your clinician or testing service specifically instructs you to do so.

Can I book an appointment here?

The appointment page is a demonstration form. It does not send requests or reserve a time until a booking service is connected.

What if I have urgent symptoms?

For severe chest pain, severe breathlessness, fainting, or suspected stroke, seek emergency medical help immediately. Do not wait for an online response.
